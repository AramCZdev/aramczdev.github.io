About

Hey, I'm Aram — a self-taught developer and game developer from the Czech Republic. I spend most of my time making games in Godot, breaking things on Linux, and occasionally fixing them (success rate varies).

I'm deeply passionate about game development and programming. I'm still learning new things constantly, but I've picked up enough Godot, and system administration to know what I'm doing... most of the time. I believe in learning by doing, failing publicly, and shipping things that work.

Right now, I'm focused on getting Hell Jump to full release in May 2026 — a hellish platformer that's been my playground for learning Godot while pushing myself to ship a polished product. Beyond games, I enjoy building tools, exploring new tech, and contributing to projects that interest me.

If you're into indie games, Godot, Linux, or just want to chat about terrible life choices, hit me up.

Also when I am not programming I yell to my code at 3 AM. Just kidding lol
